Privacy Policy

Application: Fan Remote for Goldmedal
Last updated: September 12, 2026
Summary: Fan Remote for Goldmedal is an entirely offline utility. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers whatsoever.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Fan Remote for Goldmedal (the "Application"), developed by Sajarin M. The Application is designed to control compatible infrared-capable ceiling fans using your device's built-in infrared (IR) transmitter.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • No Personal Information: We do not require, collect, or store any personal data such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or phone numbers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The Application contains no third-party tracking SDKs, advertising libraries, crash reporting tools, or behavioral cookies.
  • No Internet Permission: The Application does not request or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The Android operating system physically prevents the application from making any network connections.

3. Device Permissions

The Application requests only a single Android permission:

  • android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR: This permission is required solely to emit physical infrared light signals via your phone's built-in IR blaster to the ceiling fan when a button is pressed. This action happens strictly locally in real time and does not record, log, or transmit any data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect or process any data whatsoever, it does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application does not share data with, connect to, or integrate with any third-party services or data brokers.
