# Privacy Policy for Fan Remote for Goldmedal

**Last updated:** September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices of **Fan Remote for Goldmedal** (the "Application"), developed by **Sajarin M** ("we", "us", or "our").

## Overview

Fan Remote for Goldmedal is an offline utility designed to control compatible infrared-capable ceiling fans using an Android device's built-in infrared (IR) transmitter.

We believe in complete privacy. **The Application does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or device data whatsoever.**

## Information Collection and Use

* **No Personal Data Collected:** The Application does not require user accounts, logins, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or contact lists.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** The Application contains no analytics SDKs, advertising libraries, crash reporting trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.
* **No Internet Access:** The Application does not request or possess the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission. The Android operating system sandbox strictly prevents the Application from initiating or receiving any network communication.

## Device Permissions

The Application requests only one permission:

* **`android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR`:** Required solely to transmit consumer infrared signals via your phone's built-in IR blaster to the ceiling fan when you press a remote key. This permission is used exclusively for instantaneous physical signal transmission and does not record or store any data.

## Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate with, link to, or share data with any third-party services, APIs, or ad networks.
